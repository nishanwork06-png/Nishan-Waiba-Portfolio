Craftopedia — Social Media Campaign

An industry-oriented IIDE project: audited Craftopedia's social presence, mapped the competitive landscape and built a paid + organic plan for the brand.

Full campaign blueprint with personas, content calendar, KPIs and budget allocation

Challenge
Craftopedia had inconsistent posting and no clear positioning against competitors, so its handmade-craft audience never saw a coherent brand story.
Approach
Audited the existing profiles, benchmarked three competitors, built buyer personas, then designed a monthly content calendar paired with a Meta ads plan and budget split across awareness and conversion.
Result
Delivered a campaign blueprint with defined KPIs and budget allocation, ready to run.

Social Media Audit & Engagement Analysis

Analysed engagement patterns across Meta and YouTube to identify what content earned reach, and translated the findings into content and growth opportunities.

Prioritised list of content and growth opportunities backed by engagement data

Challenge
The brand posted frequently but couldn't explain which formats actually drove reach and saves, so budget kept going to the wrong content.
Approach
Exported posts of Meta and YouTube data, scored each by reach, engagement rate and watch time, then clustered the winners by format, topic and posting time.
Result
A ranked opportunity list showing the formats worth doubling down on.

  • YouTube Analytics

hoop — Search Marketing Campaign

A technical SEO audit for hoop, covering site performance, crawlability and indexation, then keyword research and search-intent mapping to reshape the product and content strategy.

Audit findings translated into a prioritised on-page and content roadmap

Challenge
hoop's pages were slow and partly un-indexed, and the keyword set didn't match how buyers actually searched for the product.
Approach
Ran a full crawl and Core Web Vitals review, logged indexation gaps, then built a keyword map grouped by search intent across the funnel.
Result
A prioritised roadmap sequencing technical fixes ahead of content work.
  • Technical SEO
  • Keyword Research
  • Google Search Console

On-Page & Content Strategy for hoop

Optimised title tags, meta descriptions, heading structure, schema markup and image alt text, then built a content strategy of topic clusters, internal linking and SEO blog recommendations.

Off-page plan with backlink outreach, KPI tracking and Search Console measurement

Challenge
Ranking pages were being under-clicked: titles and descriptions were generic, and blog posts sat as isolated pages with no internal linking.
Approach
Rewrote metadata and heading hierarchy, added schema markup and alt text, then grouped content into topic clusters with pillar pages and an internal linking plan.
Result
An on-page and off-page playbook with backlink outreach and Search Console tracking.
